## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a gutter repair cost in Bath?**
A resealed cast iron joint or refitted bracket is around £100 to £180. Patching a parapet gutter lead lining or repairing a corroded cast iron section is typically £250 to £600, more if access is difficult.

**My ceiling is staining below the parapet - is that the gutter?**
Very likely. A blocked or split lead-lined parapet gutter sends water inward under the roof rather than over the front, showing as ceiling stains near the front wall. It needs prompt attention.

**Can I put uPVC gutters on my Bath house?**
On a rear elevation, often yes. On a street-facing or listed frontage, no - cast iron or cast aluminium is expected, and uPVC can breach listed-building consent.
