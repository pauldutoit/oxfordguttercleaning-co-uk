## Frequently asked questions

**How much is gutter cleaning in Bath?**
An Oldfield Park or Twerton terrace is around £70 to £95. A Georgian townhouse with concealed parapet gutters needs a proper inspection and clean and is typically £150 to £300 depending on access and roof size.

**What is a parapet gutter and why does it matter?**
It is a gutter running behind the front parapet wall of a Georgian terrace, lined in lead, invisible from the street. When it blocks, water backs up under the roof and comes through ceilings - so it needs checking yearly, not just when there is a visible problem.

**Can you work on a listed building in central Bath?**
Cleaning needs no consent. Our specialists know not to disturb historic lead or stonework and will flag anything that would need listed-building consent before repair.
