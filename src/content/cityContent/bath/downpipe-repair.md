On Bath's Georgian terraces the downpipes are often cast iron, running from concealed parapet outlets down the rear or side, and they block at the top bend or a rusted-through swan-neck. Elsewhere - Oldfield Park, Twerton, the post-war estates - it is more standard uPVC and cast iron on eaves outlets.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much to clear a blocked downpipe in Bath?**
A straightforward eaves downpipe clear is £65 to £110. A parapet-fed cast iron pipe, or a blockage down in an underground gulley, is more like £120 to £200.

**Water is running down the outside of my cast iron downpipe - why?**
Usually a blocked or split socket joint higher up, or the pipe backing up from a blockage below. Both are repairable without replacing the whole pipe.

**Could a leaking downpipe be causing damp inside?**
Yes - it is one of the most common causes of penetrating damp on Bath stone walls, which are porous and hold water. Worth fixing before it spreads.
