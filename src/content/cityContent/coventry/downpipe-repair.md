## Downpipe clearing and repair across Coventry

A blocked downpipe on a Coventry semi shows up fast - water backs up and spills over the top of the hopper head or splits a joint lower down, often soaking the render right where it meets the pavement. ## Frequently asked questions

**How much does downpipe repair cost in Coventry?**
Clearing a blocked downpipe typically costs £55-£90, while resealing or replacing a short damaged section runs £100-£180. A full downpipe replacement, including a period-style cast-iron profile, can reach £250-£270.

**Why does my downpipe keep blocking in the same spot?**
Usually there's a tight bend or offset in the pipe run that catches leaves and debris - once cleared, fitting a simple balloon guard at the gutter outlet often stops it recurring.

**Can a cracked cast-iron downpipe be repaired instead of replaced?**
Yes, in most cases - a short repair collar or resin seal on a hairline crack is far cheaper than a full swap, provided the corrosion hasn't spread along the whole length.
