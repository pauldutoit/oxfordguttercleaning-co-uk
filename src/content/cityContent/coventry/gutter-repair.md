## Fixing leaks and sagging runs on Coventry's post-war housing stock

A huge share of Coventry's housing was built fast in the two decades after the war, and a lot of it still carries the original steel or early plastic guttering that's now reaching the end of its life - brackets pull away from timber fascias, joints crack in the cold, and a single loose bracket on a long unbroken run can bring the whole length down over a doorway. Repairing rather than replacing the full run is usually the cheaper, faster fix if it's caught early.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does gutter repair cost in Coventry?**
A single leaking joint or bracket typically runs £70-£110, while a longer sagging section needing several new brackets and reseating is closer to £150-£250. Full-length steel replacement on an older property can reach £350-£400.

**Can a sagging gutter be fixed without replacing the whole run?**
Usually yes. If the gutter material itself is sound, refitting brackets at closer spacing and resealing joints solves most sag - full replacement is only needed when the gutter has cracked or the fascia behind it has rotted.

**What causes gutters to pull away from the house in Coventry?**
Ice expansion in winter and the weight of wet leaves from the city's mature street trees are the two most common causes, especially on the longer unbroken runs found on 1960s semis.
