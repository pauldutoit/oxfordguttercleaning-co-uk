## Frequently asked questions

**How much does full gutter replacement cost in Coventry?**
A terraced house typically runs £420-£650 in uPVC, a semi-detached property £650-£950, and a larger detached house or one with cast-iron replacement can reach £1,000-£1,450 depending on length and finish.

**Is planning permission needed to replace gutters in Coventry?**
Standard uPVC replacement rarely needs permission, but on listed buildings or within a conservation area such as parts of Chapelfields, like-for-like cast-iron or a close match may be required - a local installer will know which streets apply.

**How long does uPVC guttering last compared to the original steel?**
uPVC typically lasts 20-25 years virtually maintenance-free, well beyond the 30-40 years steel achieved before it started needing the frequent repairs common on Coventry's post-war stock today.
