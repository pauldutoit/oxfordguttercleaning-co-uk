## Clearing and repairing downpipes across Reading

A downpipe that overflows at the top, drips at every collar, or pours water against the wall is usually blocked at a bend or an underground gulley, or has a split socket. On Reading's older terraces the blockage is often moss and grit from the gutter; on the newer Earley and Woodley estates it is more often a silted-up below-ground drain.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much to clear a blocked downpipe in Reading?**
A simple clear is £60 to £95. If it needs rods or a jet down into an underground gulley, £110 to £175.

**The downpipe overflows at the top in heavy rain - why?**
Almost always a blockage further down backing the water up. Clearing it usually solves the overflow with no new parts.

**Can a leaking downpipe damage my house?**
Yes - a pipe leaking against a wall is a leading cause of penetrating damp on Reading's older solid-wall terraces. Best dealt with quickly.
