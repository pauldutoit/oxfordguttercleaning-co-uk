## Frequently asked questions

**What does a gutter repair cost in Reading?**
A resealed joint or refitted bracket is usually £85 to £150. Realigning a sagging run or swapping a damaged section runs £180 to £450 depending on height and length.

**My 1990s uPVC gutters sag between the clips - repair or replace?**
If the plastic isn't brittle or cracked, adding brackets and realigning is a cheap fix that buys years. Once uPVC has gone brittle and yellow, replacement is better value.

**Can you match my existing gutter colour?**
Usually yes - black, white, brown and anthracite in standard profiles are all readily matched. Older or unusual shades may mean a full-run swap for consistency.
