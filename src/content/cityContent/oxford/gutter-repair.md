## Frequently asked questions

**How much does a gutter repair cost in Oxford?**
Simple fixes - a resealed joint or a refitted bracket - run around £90 to £150. Replacing a corroded cast iron section or realigning a full sagging run is more like £200 to £500 depending on height and access.

**Is it worth repairing old cast iron guttering?**
Usually yes if the bulk of the run is sound. Cast iron lasts decades once descaled, primed and repainted, and it keeps the right look on a period Oxford terrace. Full replacement only makes sense once corrosion is widespread.

**Can you fix a leak that only shows in heavy rain?**
Yes - specialists will run water through the suspect section to trace an intermittent overflow or hairline split rather than guessing.
