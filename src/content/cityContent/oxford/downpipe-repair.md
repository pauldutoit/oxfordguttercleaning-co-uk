## Clearing and repairing blocked downpipes across Oxford

A downpipe that gurgles, overflows at the top or leaks at every joint is usually blocked at a bend or a ground-level shoe, or has a cracked socket. On Oxford's older terraces the culprit is often a compacted plug of moss and grit washed down from the gutter, or a below-ground gulley silted up with leaf mulch.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much to clear a blocked downpipe in Oxford?**
A straightforward clear is usually £60 to £100. If the blockage is in an underground gulley or needs rods and a jet, expect £110 to £180. Replacing a cracked cast iron socket costs more.

**Why does my downpipe leak only at the top?**
That's a classic sign the pipe is blocked lower down and backing up. Clearing the blockage usually stops the leak without any new parts.

**Can a leaking downpipe cause damp inside?**
Yes - a downpipe leaking against a wall is one of the most common causes of penetrating damp on Oxford's solid-wall terraces. Worth fixing promptly.
