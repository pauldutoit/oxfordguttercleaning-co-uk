When a run is beyond repair - widespread corrosion, brittle sun-damaged uPVC, or simply the wrong profile for the roof - a full replacement is the sensible call. ## Frequently asked questions

**How much is a full gutter replacement in Oxford?**
A single-storey uPVC run on a semi starts around £520 to £900. A two-storey terrace in cast aluminium is typically £1,100 to £1,900, and cast iron more again. Access and the number of downpipes are the main variables.

**Do I need cast iron on a conservation-area house?**
Often on the street-facing elevation, yes. Cast aluminium is usually accepted as a visually identical, far lighter alternative. Rear elevations can often take uPVC - a local fitter will know your area's rules.

**How long does a replacement take?**
Most houses are a one to two-day job once any scaffold is up. Larger period properties with multiple elevations can run to three or four days.
