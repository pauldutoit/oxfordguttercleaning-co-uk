## Frequently asked questions

**How much does downpipe repair cost in Northampton?**
Clearing a blocked downpipe typically costs £55-£90, resealing or replacing a damaged section £100-£175, and full replacement including a period-matched profile around £250-£275.

**How do I know if a downpipe is causing damp inside my house?**
A vertical damp stain on an internal wall roughly in line with where the downpipe runs outside is the classic sign, especially on older terraces where the pipe sits close to the brickwork.

**Can cast-iron downpipes on a Northampton terrace be repaired rather than replaced?**
Often yes, if caught before the corrosion spreads too far - a repair collar or resin seal on a localised split is much cheaper than a full swap and keeps the original look.
