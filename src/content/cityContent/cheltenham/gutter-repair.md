## Frequently asked questions

**What does a gutter repair cost in Cheltenham?**
A resealed joint or refitted bracket is around £85 to £150. Cast iron section repair or realigning a full sagging run is £180 to £450, and parapet gutter lead repairs typically £250 to £550.

**My ceiling is marking near the front wall - could it be the gutter?**
On a Regency terrace with a parapet gutter, very possibly. A blocked or split lead lining pushes water inward under the roof rather than over the front. It is worth getting looked at before it spreads.

**Is uPVC an option on my terrace?**
On the rear, usually. On a street-facing Regency frontage in a conservation area, cast iron or cast aluminium is expected instead.
