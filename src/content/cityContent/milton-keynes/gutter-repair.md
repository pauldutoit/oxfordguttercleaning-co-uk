## Frequently asked questions

**How much does gutter repair cost in Milton Keynes?**
A single union or outlet repair typically costs £80-£120, while a longer section needing several new brackets runs £140-£260. More extensive work on an older village property can reach £350-£420.

**Why do so many MK gutters fail at the joints rather than crack outright?**
The wide uPVC profile used across most estate housing expands and contracts significantly with temperature, which over 20-30 years works the union seals loose before the gutter material itself fails.

**Can a leaking union be fixed without replacing the whole gutter run?**
Yes in almost all cases - refitting or resealing the union is a same-day fix, and only becomes a full replacement job if the surrounding gutter has also cracked or sagged.
