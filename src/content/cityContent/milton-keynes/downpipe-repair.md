## Frequently asked questions

**How much does downpipe repair cost in Milton Keynes?**
Clearing a blocked downpipe typically costs £60-£95, resealing or replacing a damaged section £110-£190, and a full downpipe replacement on an estate property around £270-£300.

**Why do downpipes on MK estates block more than on older housing?**
The wide, flat rooflines shed a large volume of water and debris through comparatively few downpipes, so leaf litter from the extensive redway tree planting concentrates faster than on a traditional pitched-roof terrace.

**Can a shared party-wall downpipe be repaired without disturbing the neighbouring property?**
In most cases yes - repairs are done from the outside without needing access inside either house, though it's worth mentioning to a neighbour if scaffolding or a tower is needed.
