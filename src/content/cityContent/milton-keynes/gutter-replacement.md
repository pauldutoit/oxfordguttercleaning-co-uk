**How much does full gutter replacement cost in Milton Keynes?**
A standard estate terrace or townhouse runs roughly £500-£750 in uPVC, a semi or detached house £750-£1,150, and larger properties or those needing cast-iron-style finishes can reach £1,400-£1,700.

**Is it worth upgrading to a larger gutter profile on replacement?**
Often yes on MK's flatter, wider rooflines - a higher-capacity profile costs a little more upfront but copes better with heavy downpours and reduces overflow at the joints, a common complaint on the original 1970s-80s fitting.
