## Frequently asked questions

**How much does gutter repair cost in Slough?**
A single leaking joint or bracket typically costs £80-£120, a longer sagging section £150-£270, and more extensive repair on an older semi up to £420.

**Why do bay-window junctions need extra care when repairing gutters?**
The angled bay roofline on many 1930s Slough semis creates a junction point where two gutter runs meet, and that's usually where leaks or sagging show up first.

**Can uPVC guttering fitted decades ago still be repaired?**
Often yes - if the material itself hasn't gone brittle, refitting brackets and resealing joints is usually cheaper than a full replacement.
